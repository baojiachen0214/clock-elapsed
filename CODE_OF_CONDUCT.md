# Code of Conduct

Clock Elapsed welcomes contributors of every background and level of experience.

Be respectful, give specific and constructive feedback, and discuss the work rather than the person. Respect privacy and ask before sharing someone else's personal information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact.

To report a concern privately, contact the maintainer through the contact information on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/baojiachen0214). Include relevant links and only the personal information needed to understand the report. Reports will be handled with care; an immediate response cannot be guaranteed.
